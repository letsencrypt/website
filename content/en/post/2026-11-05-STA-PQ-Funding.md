## Merkle Tree Certificates

Merkle Tree Certificates are a new approach that adds post-quantum authentication to the web without sacrificing the speed and reliability that have made TLS universal. Replacing today’s certificate signatures with post-quantum equivalents would push a single TLS handshake well past 10 kilobytes, slowing connections and causing some to fail; MTCs keep the handshake small by covering a whole batch of certificates with one signature.

This investment supports ISRG’s efforts to [prepare Let's Encrypt](/2026/06/03/pq-certs) for the post-quantum era by researching, developing, and deploying post-quantum Merkle Tree Certificates across its Certificate Authority infrastructure. Through 2027, this initiative will help ISRG:

- Contribute to standardization of Merkle Tree Certificate Authorities at the IETF, the body that develops the Internet’s technical standards; in C2SP, a community project that publishes precise specifications for cryptographic protocols; and with root programs, the policies that decide which certificate authorities browsers and operating systems trust.

- Transition the Merkle Tree Certificate Authority from initial standardization and staging tests to a fully reliable, compliant production launch.

- Develop documentation, conduct outreach, and roll out Merkle Tree Certificates globally to subscribers.
